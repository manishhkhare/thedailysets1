FOCUS: NATURAL HAIR GROWTH 🌱

7 Habits for Healthier Hair

Focus on nutrition, scalp health, gentle care and consistency instead of looking for overnight results.

01

Maintain a Healthy Diet

Follow a balanced, nutrient-rich diet. Avoid excessive oily foods and junk food. Include fresh fruits, vegetables and whole foods in your daily meals.

02

Consume Enough Protein

Include eggs, paneer, milk, lentils, soy chunks and other suitable protein-rich foods. Protein supports normal hair structure.

03

Maintain Vitamin & Mineral Levels

Important nutrients include Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Folate B9, Iron, Zinc and Biotin B7.

Vitamin D B12 Iron Zinc Biotin
04

Keep Your Scalp Clean & Healthy

Maintain good scalp hygiene. Wash your hair regularly according to your scalp type. Keep your scalp free from excessive buildup.

05

Avoid Excessive Heat & Harsh Products

Minimize excessive heat-styling tools. Avoid harsh hair products and handle hair gently to reduce breakage.

06

Follow a Consistent Hair Care Routine

Establish a simple routine. Choose products that suit your scalp and hair type. Be patient and consistent.

07

Monitor Hairline & Hair Shedding

Track changes in your hairline and excessive shedding. Take progress photos periodically using similar lighting and angles.

Important Note

These habits support general hair and scalp health, but cannot guarantee that hair loss will stop or hair will regrow. Persistent, sudden or severe hair shedding should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
